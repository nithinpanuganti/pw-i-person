Hotspot Ranking Formulation

Hotspot score index is computed transparently without external GIS or maps APIs:

Hotspot Score = (Open Reports × 10) + (Critical Reports × 15) + (High Reports × 8) + (Total Reports × 2)

CivicLens AI Intelligence Methodology & Limitations

1. Heuristic Classification: Uses text normalization, token boundary matching, and weighted term frequency dictionaries across 7 civic domains. Does not call third-party Large Language Models or cloud AI APIs.

2. Explainable Priority Scoring: Transparent arithmetic weighting based on reported severity (+10 to +35), safety-critical hazard tokens (+8 to +25), accessibility barriers (+12), and recurring locality clusters (+6 per unresolved neighbor report).

3. Limitations & Disclaimers:

  • Keyword rules cannot comprehend all nuanced real-world contexts. Users can manually override suggested categories.
  • Priority scores are community decision-support suggestions, NOT verified municipal assessments or police/fire emergency dispatches.
  • All observations are stored exclusively within the local browser and require manual export to share.

Action Center — Drive Community Impact

Automated, rule-based recommendations that translate recurring problems into concrete citizen initiatives, petitions, and checklists.

How Action Recommendations are Generated

Recommendations are dynamically triggered when specific criteria are satisfied in the local dataset:

  • Sanitation Cleanups: Triggered when 2 or more unresolved Waste Management reports occur in the same locality.
  • Road Repair Dossiers: Synthesized when multiple pothole or asphalt defects affect pedestrian or school routes.
  • Drainage Audits: Formulated when water leaks or culvert blockages create flooding or health risks.
  • Accessibility Walkthroughs: Created when wheelchair ramps or pedestrian signals are reported nonfunctional.
